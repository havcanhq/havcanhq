Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

HAVCAN (“HAVCAN”, “we”, “us”) is a managed creative services platform. Customers request creative work, and our team coordinates it and communicates with them, including through WhatsApp. This policy explains what personal information we handle, why, and the choices you have. It applies to this website and app and to our WhatsApp Business communications.

1. Information we collect

Account and profile information

Your name, email address and phone number when you enter them in your profile or share them with us to place or manage a request.

Project and order data

The service you choose, your project requirements and briefs, reference material you share, order status, deliverables and revision feedback.

WhatsApp messages and media

When you contact HAVCAN on WhatsApp, we receive your WhatsApp phone number, profile name (if WhatsApp provides it), message content, timestamps, delivery status, and any media you send, such as images, documents, audio or video. We also store the replies we send you.

Contact information

Details you give us when you reach out by WhatsApp or through our social channels so we can respond to you.

Technical and device data

Team members who sign in to the operator inbox use a session cookie so they stay signed in. Your browser may also store app preferences and drafts locally on your device (see section 5). Our hosting and infrastructure providers may process technical data such as IP address and browser type as part of delivering the site.

2. How we use information

  • To understand, quote, deliver and revise the creative work you request.
  • To communicate with you about your projects, orders and support questions.
  • To let authorized HAVCAN team members view and reply to WhatsApp conversations in our inbox.
  • To operate, secure and troubleshoot the service and prevent misuse.
  • To meet legal obligations and enforce our terms.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use WhatsApp message content for advertising.

3. WhatsApp Business Cloud API

HAVCAN uses the WhatsApp Business Platform (Cloud API) provided by Meta to send and receive messages. Messages you send to our WhatsApp Business number are delivered to our backend, where they are stored so our team can read them, reply, and keep the project history together with your order. Media files you send are retrieved through the Cloud API and made available to our team in the same conversation.

We message you only in response to your contact or in connection with your request, and in line with WhatsApp’s business policies. Meta processes WhatsApp data under its own terms and privacy policy; see WhatsApp Privacy Policy. Messages you send to HAVCAN can be read by our team, so please do not share sensitive information such as passwords or payment card details.

4. Data storage and hosting

This website is served through Cloudflare. Application data, including account details, order records and WhatsApp conversations, is handled by our backend service hosted on Railway. Data may be processed in countries other than your own, depending on where these providers operate.

5. Local browser storage and cookies

The app saves some preferences and data, such as profile drafts and recent searches, in your browser’s local storage on your device. Team sign-in uses a session cookie. We do not use this storage for advertising. You can clear it at any time in your browser settings; some features may reset as a result.

6. Third-party services

  • Meta / WhatsApp – message delivery and media through the WhatsApp Business Cloud API.
  • Cloudflare – website hosting and delivery.
  • Railway – backend hosting for application data.
  • Google Fonts, jsDelivr, Tailwind CSS CDN and Simple Icons CDN – fonts, scripts and icons loaded when you open the site; these providers may receive your IP address and browser details.
  • Instagram, YouTube and X – only if you follow links to our profiles; their own policies then apply.

We share information with these providers only as needed to run the service, or where required by law. We do not sell or rent your data to third parties.

7. Security

We use reasonable measures to protect your information, including HTTPS for data in transit and sign-in protected access for our team’s inbox. No online service can be guaranteed completely secure, so we cannot promise absolute security.

8. Data retention and deletion

We keep project, order and WhatsApp conversation data for as long as needed to deliver your work, support you, and meet legal or accounting obligations. After that, we delete or anonymize it.

How to request deletion of your data

Send us a message from your WhatsApp number to +91 9631126328 with the words “Delete my data”. We may ask you to confirm you own the number. We will then delete your profile details, project and order records, and WhatsApp conversation history and media that we hold, except anything we are legally required to keep. Deleting messages on your own phone does not delete our copy, and Meta keeps its own records under its policies.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live and applicable law, you may have the right to:

  • Ask what personal information we hold about you and get a copy.
  • Ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Ask us to delete your information.
  • Withdraw consent where we rely on it, and object to or limit certain uses.
  • Stop WhatsApp messages at any time by telling us to stop or by blocking the number.

To exercise any of these, contact us using the details below.

10. Children

HAVCAN is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date above shows the latest version. Material changes will be reflected on this page.

12. Contact us

For privacy questions or requests, contact HAVCAN on WhatsApp at +91 9631126328, or message us on Instagram @havcanhq.
